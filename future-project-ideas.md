# Future Vibe-Coding Project Ideas

1. **Tiny Aquarium** — Create a relaxing aquarium where fish swim around plants and decorations. Add a few fish personalities, a day/night cycle, and simple feeding interactions.
2. **Mini Golf Course Designer** — Build a small mini-golf game with a course editor. Let players place walls and obstacles, then aim and set the power of each shot.
3. **Pixel Art Studio** — Make a browser-based pixel editor with a resizable grid, color palette, undo, and PNG export.
4. **Pocket Planet Explorer** — Generate tiny planets with different terrain, weather, and creatures. Let visitors rotate a planet and discover what lives there.
5. **One-Button Space Dodger** — Make an arcade game where a spaceship dodges asteroids using one-button controls. Add a score streak and increasingly tricky waves.
6. **Procedural Music Toy** — Create a colorful step sequencer that turns a grid of clicks into a looping tune. Include a few instruments and tempo controls.
7. **Cozy Garden Planner** — Let users arrange flowers, paths, and garden decorations on a small plot. Add seasonal palettes and a way to save or share a design.
8. **Daily Brain-Teaser Generator** — Offer a fresh little logic or pattern puzzle each day, with hints, a timer, and a streak counter.
9. **Dream Journal Map** — Turn short dream notes into a personal map of recurring places, characters, and themes. Keep entries private in the browser.
10. **Color Palette Playground** — Generate harmonious color palettes and preview them on a sample poster or webpage. Let users lock favorite colors and copy the palette.
